## Core Facts (Content Reference)

These points should be reflected consistently across the website:

### Philosophy
- **Scarcity**: prioritizes limited availability over rapid transactions to retain intrinsic value. 
- **Provenance**: emphasizes history/authenticity and reinforces trust and worth. 
- “A Different Philosophy” focused on **value over speculation**.

### What AurynX Is
- **Fixed supply / non-inflationary** designed to preserve value through scarcity. 
- **Solana-based**, leveraging efficient transactions and provenance tracking. 
- **Private issuance** to select sophisticated participants (e.g., curated collectors/family offices).

### What AurynX Is Not
- **Not equity**: no ownership rights, profit claims, or dividends.
- **Not yield-bearing**: no interest/yield returns; intended for appreciation as a collectible (not income). 

### Supply
- **Total supply is fixed and immutable: 1,000,000,000 tokens.**

### Allocation & Holding Orientation
- Allocation described as **Private, Curated, Limited tranches, and Discretionary**.
- Holding orientation: **Long-term**, **Illiquid by design**, **Transfer-restricted initially**.

### Risk Considerations
- **Speculative nature**: value may fluctuate significantly.
- **Regulatory risk**: evolving regulations may affect treatment and transactions.
- **Technology risk**: cybersecurity and platform reliability considerations.

### Intended Holders
- Target audience includes **Family Offices**, **UHNW collectors**, and **Institutional participants**.
